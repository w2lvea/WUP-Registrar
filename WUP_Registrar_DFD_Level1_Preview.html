6 Sub-Processes • 4 External Entities • 5 Data Stores
👤 EXTERNAL ACTORS
🎓 Student ACTOR
Role: University students requiring documents (Transcript of Records, Grades, Enrollment).

Inputs Provided: • Login credentials & OTP
• Official Receipt (OR) Number
• Clearance & Assessment Slips
• Requested document types & copies

Outputs Received: • Reference Tracking Number
• Live status & Claim Pickup Date
🏛️ Cashier / Treasury ACTOR
Role: University Finance & Treasury Office.

Inputs Provided: • Official Receipts (OR) payment records.
• Validates student payments.

Outputs: • Printed physical OR given to student.
💼 Registrar Staff ACTOR
Role: Office of Records Personnel.

Inputs Provided: • Approvals / Declines
• Pickup Claim Dates
• Price & Catalog updates

Outputs Received: • Dashboard with pending requests & photos.
📲 Notification Gateways SERVICE
Role: External communication channels.

• Semaphore: SMS OTP sender.
• EmailJS: Email OTP sender.
• Sends 6-digit codes for recovery.
⚙️ CORE SUB-PROCESSES (The System Engines)
PROCESS 1.0
User Authentication & Password Recovery
Verifies student/staff credentials against encrypted hashes, issues secure HTTP-only session cookies, forces first-time password changes, and dispatches 6-digit OTP codes via SMS or Email for account recovery.
📥 In: Username & Password 🗄️ Store: D1 User Accounts 📤 Out: Session Token / OTP Code
PROCESS 2.0
Receipt & Payment Validation
Checks the student's entered Official Receipt (OR) Number against Cashier records in the database. Verifies payment amount and strictly enforces the single-use rule (a receipt cannot be used more than once).
📥 In: OR Number & Student ID 🗄️ Store: D2 Official Receipts 📤 Out: Receipt Validity Status
PROCESS 3.0
Document Request Submission & Filing
Bundles the student's requested documents (e.g. 1 Transcript of Records + 2 Certificate of Grades), uploaded assessment slips, and clearance photos into an active request ticket with a unique reference number (e.g. REQ-2026-001).
📥 In: Selected Documents & Photos 🗄️ Store: D3 Requests & D4 Timeline 📤 Out: Tracking Reference #
PROCESS 4.0
Request Review & Document Processing
Registrar officers inspect the submitted clearance photos and Treasury receipts. They advance the request through stages: Pending Review ➔ Processing ➔ Ready, assign a scheduled pickup Claim Date, or Decline with a written explanation.
📥 In: Staff Decision & Claim Date 🗄️ Store: D3 Requests & D4 Timeline 📤 Out: Status Update Notification
PROCESS 5.0
Status Tracking & Document Pickup
Displays real-time timeline milestones for the student to follow every step. When the student arrives on their scheduled claim date, the registrar marks the document as Completed / Claimed.
📥 In: Reference Code Lookup 🗄️ Store: D4 Request Timeline 📤 Out: Milestone Journey & Pickup Notice
PROCESS 6.0
Catalog & Clearance Fees Management
Registrar administrators manage which document types are currently available, set processing lead times (e.g. 5-7 days for TOR), and maintain the official university clearance fees price schedule.
📥 In: Price & Catalog Edits 🗄️ Store: D5 Documents & Fees 📤 Out: Updated Catalog to Form
🗄️ DATA STORES (Databases)
D1 User Accounts & Sessions
Stores student profiles, registrar staff accounts, active login cookies, and password recovery OTP PINs.
D2 Official Receipts Store
Treasury cashier database storing issued OR numbers, student names, amounts paid, and issue dates.
D3 Document Requests & Items
Active requests, uploaded clearance photos, document items list, copy counts, and assigned claim dates.
D4 Request Timeline & Audit
Chronological milestone log of each step (Submitted, Verified, Processing, Ready, Claimed, Declined).
D5 Documents & Fees Catalog
University reference catalog of requestable certificates, lead times, and official fee prices.
